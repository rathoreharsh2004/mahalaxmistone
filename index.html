QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information about our natural limestone, custom processing, and delivery.

What makes Bethamcherla & Tandur limestone distinct from other stones? +

Bethamcherla and Tandur limestone belong to dense sediment geological formations with exceptional compressive strength, low water absorption (<0.5%), and natural non-porous structure. Unlike artificial vitrified tiles, limestone breathes naturally, resists algae formation, and develops a richer patina with age.

Can Kadapa Black or Tandur Yellow be used for outdoor pool decks? +

Yes, absolutely. For pool decks and wet pathways, we recommend our Natural Cleft or River Wash finish. These textures provide a natural R11 slip rating that prevents slips even when wet, while Tandur Yellow naturally repels thermal heat, staying pleasant under bare feet.

What standard thicknesses and slab sizes do you manufacture? +

Our standard calibrated thicknesses are 20mm, 25mm, 30mm, and 40mm. Standard sizes include 30x30 cm, 60x30 cm, 60x60 cm, and 90x60 cm. We also supply customized cut-to-size slabs, stair treads with bullnosed edges, and driveway cobbles.

How do you handle Pan-India transportation and packaging? +

All finished slabs and tiles are packed vertically inside fumigated, heavy-duty wooden crates with foam cushioning between delicate edges. We coordinate full truckloads (FTL) and partial truckloads (PTL) across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and nationwide.

Can I request sample pieces before ordering full quantities? +

Yes. Architects, designers, and project managers can request physical sample boxes via courier. Simply reach out to us on WhatsApp with your required stone varieties and shipping address.
